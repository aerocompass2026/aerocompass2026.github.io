ABOUT THE COMPANY

AeroCompass Worldwide Freightlines

أيرو كومباس للشحن العالمي — شركة لوجستية سعودية

A Saudi freight forwarding, warehousing and cargo logistics company with its head office in Riyadh and branches in Jeddah (Gulf Center) and Dammam. AeroCompass brings together the experience of its parent groups, Art Map Engineering Consultants, Flydana and WAJA, in one dedicated freight company that supports the goals of Saudi Vision 2030.

We specialise in high-security, highly regulated and time-sensitive cargo, from everyday freight to defence logistics.

What we do

Core services

  • Air and sea freight forwarding, import and export
  • Transshipment and cross-docking
  • Customs clearance at all major Saudi ports and borders
  • Warehousing and storage, dry and ambient
  • Domestic and international land transport

Value-added services

  • SABER certificate facilitation
  • Marine and cargo insurance
  • Professional packaging and crating

AeroCompass Industrial Logistics

Our industrial and project division: project and heavy cargo, out-of-gauge (OOG) transport, container stuffing and lashing, warehousing and 3PL, terminal services, and industrial packing, for forwarders, EPC contractors and industrial customers across the Kingdom.
